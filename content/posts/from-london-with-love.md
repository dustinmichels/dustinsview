<p>I walk in London<br>
Among immense, impossible<br>
spirals of glass, and fantastic, slanting<br>
protrusions of office-bearing stone<br>
Impressive testaments to the city’s&nbsp;ingenuity, modernity<br>
–Or, rather, its wealth</p>
<p>Magnificent, ostentatious temples<br>
like those of Ancient Greeks and Egyptians before</p>
<p>Power symbols of the rich<br>
containing the misery of the poor</p>
<p>When will we learn to wear simple clothes?</p>
<p>When will we measure the strength of a society<br>
Not by the grandeur of its elements<br>
But by the scarcity of its pain?</p>
<p>In thinking of future cities<br>
We must let our imaginations pan down<br>
from the flying cars and floating gardens<br>
of tomorrow’s magnificent skyline<br>
To the darkest, most neglected part of town<br>
– Is it safe there?</p>
