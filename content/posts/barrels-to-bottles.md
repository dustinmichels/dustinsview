<p style="text-align: center;">An oil rig, like a leech in the ocean<br>
Steadily slurping<br>
Thirst is never quenched<br>
A clumsy hurry, a suicidal flurry<br>
Money is driving<br>
Up this steep, windy road</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">And now a machine, a cold, fast machine<br>
Identical Parts<br>
On the assembly line<br>
Always producing, so much to be doing<br>
We need all this junk<br>
To fill space in our lives</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">And from all our efforts, behold this creation<br>
Aren’t you proud<br>
of the job that we’ve done?<br>
A quick sip of water, then into the trash can<br>
To rot forever<br>
In a hole that we dug</p>
