<p>While doing some research for a blog post that I will probably never publish, (a fate that befalls most of the blogs I start to write,) I learned that scientists have&nbsp;recently&nbsp;discovered a planet that could be habitable by humans. My initial reaction, sadly enough, was something along the lines of “hey, that’s great, because when we trash this one, we’ll have another in store.”</p>
<p>I felt that concept could make for an interesting song, and when I realized the “D” and the “g” in the planet’s name basically&nbsp;rhyme, making the name sing-able, I decided it had to be done.</p>
<p>This is my ode to the&nbsp;poetically named and utterly&nbsp;ineffable&nbsp;HD 40307g.</p>
<p>(Note: The video at the start is from “slatester,” the Slate News Chanel.)</p>
<h2><b>Lyrics:</b></h2>
<p><em>Intro</em>:</p>
<p>It’s the year 2073<br>
And the world’s no longer habitable by you or by me me<br>
So it’s pretty clear we’ll simply have to leave<br>
We’re headed for HD 40307g</p>
<p><!--more--></p>
<p><em>Verse 1:</em></p>
<p>Well we polluted all the air and water<br>
And we paved over all the soil<br>
And we chopped down the trees, and we wiped out the bees<br>
And we slurped up all the oil</p>
<p>We debated and fought<br>
Till the politicians got<br>
What the corporations paid em for<br>
And now we can’t live here anymore…</p>
<p><em>Chorus:</em></p>
<p>But we still got HD 40307g<br>
Yeah we got HD 40307g<br>
Yeah we still got HD 40307g<br>
And there’s only 42 light years in between</p>
<p><em>Verse</em>:</p>
<p>Well it’s a pity about those spacious skies<br>
And amber waves of grain<br>
But I don’t feel too much guilt, cause the things that we built<br>
Looked much better anyway<br>
We were sure living smooth<br>
For a moment or two<br>
Before the planet sent us away<br>
But hey man, that’s okay</p>
<p><em>Chorus</em>:</p>
<p>Cause we got HD 40307g<br>
Yeah we still got HD 40307g<br>
Yeah we still got HD 40307g<br>
And there’s only 42 light years in between</p>
<p><em>Bridge:</em></p>
<p>And I know<br>
We’ve been overly materialistic<br>
We’ve been stubbornly unrealistic</p>
<p>And I know<br>
Our greed and laziness is self-destructive<br>
Our way of life is utterly disruptive</p>
<p>And I know<br>
We could have learned to live sustainably<br>
If we hadn’t fought incessantly</p>
<p>And we hadn’t clung so desperately<br>
To our profits and our luxuries<br>
And we’d tried to work more as a team<br>
And now we still haven’t learned these things</p>
[Hey, there it is]
<p><em>Chorus</em>:</p>
<p>You know I’m talking bout HD 40307g<br>
Yeah I’m talkin bout HD 40307g<br>
Oh I’m talking bout HD 40307g</p>
<p>It might be exactly what we need<br>
A new start for humanity<br>
This time we’ll live in harmony<br>
With the animals and trees<br>
Yes we’ll live sustainably<br>
So our new home will always be</p>
<p>HD 40307g</p>
[Hey, I got dibs on the oil]
[Like hell you do!]
[Gun Shot]
[Screams]
<p>…Yeah, on second thought….. we’re gonna exploit this place to shit….</p>
