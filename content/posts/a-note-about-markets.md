UPPSALA, SWEDEN - <strong>“Free</strong><b> market”</b> means freedom to exploit land &amp; labor; freedom to pollute air, water, and soil; freedom for multinationals to go anywhere and do anything they want, ferociously suing governments audacious enough to encourage practices like <a href="http://www.letsrecycle.com/news/latest-news/aluminium-industry-attacks-denmark-over-packaging-tax/">recycling</a>, <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6UsHHOCH4q8">not smoking</a>, or<a href="http://thewire.in/24787/indias-solar-panel-dispute-a-need-to-look-within/"> buying local</a>, until all such “anti-competitive” policies are obliterated.
