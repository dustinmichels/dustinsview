<p>love is admiration<br>
of the essence<br>
of another</p>
<p>at best, it inspires —<br>
compels you to contort<br>
like a vine, twisting and turning<br>
as it climbs to new heights</p>
<p>love is always a delusion<br>
but it’s a healthy, vital delusion<br>
like optimism<br>
like purpose<br>
like self</p>
